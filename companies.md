# Companies to watch

`/scan` reads every opening at these companies, whatever the search terms. Many of them post only on their own
job board, so keyword searches on job sites miss them. Add your own in `profile.md` > Companies to watch, and skip
any from this list there.

Board endpoints are public JSON unless the board type says otherwise; see `sources.md` > Watched companies.

## No public board

These firms hire in Prague but have no board `/scan` can read. Check them by hand.
- Banks and fintech: Revolut, Moneta, Fio, Komerční banka, Raiffeisenbank, ČSOB, Citi, FNZ.
- Trading: RSJ, Trading Technologies (https://tradingtechnologies.com/careers/).
- Big tech: Google (https://careers.google.com/locations/prague/), Oracle, Cisco (https://jobs.cisco.com/).
- AI: Resistant AI (posts on startupjobs.cz, which `/scan` already reads).
